Design an editorial product infographic using the attached photo as the base
image: a Selva Brava aluminum sunscreen tin resting on beach sand, sea out of
focus in the background. Keep the product photo natural and unretouched —
muted colors, cool coastal light, film grain, no HDR, no oversaturation.
Color mood: teal-tinted shadows, soft golden highlights, desaturated blues
and greens (never neon, never postcard-turquoise).

LAYOUT: vertical 4:5 composition. The beach photo occupies the full canvas
with a soft warm off-white panel (#FAF8F1, slightly translucent, paper
texture) covering the left side or lower two-thirds, where the feature list
sits. The tin must remain fully visible and legible — never covered by text.

HEADER: the hand-lettered "SELVA BRAVA" wordmark (from the attached label
reference, keep it EXACT) in forest green #4C6759, followed by the title
"CARACTERÍSTICAS" in bold condensed uppercase sans-serif with wide
letter-spacing, charcoal #1E2523.

FEATURE LIST — render this EXACT Spanish text, one line each, no rewording:
- COLOR BEIGE A TRAVÉS DE INFUSIÓN CON CÚRCUMA ORGÁNICA
- PROPIEDADES REGENERADORAS: MANTECA DE KARITÉ Y CACAO ORGÁNICAS

Each feature preceded by a small minimal line icon (thin stroke, rounded
caps, botanical-outdoor style: turmeric root, seeds/leaf, water drop, wave,
atom, coconut, shield) drawn in a single color — sage green #A9B59D or slate
#687978 — inside a small circle. NO filled colorful icons, NO emoji.

FOOTER: small technical line in monospace font, slate gray:
"LATA 20 GRS · Ø 5 CM · HABILITADO POR ANMAT". Small "5 cm" measurement mark
with thin bracket lines next to the tin.

STYLE RULES (brand manual): warm off-white/bone and cream backgrounds only —
never pure white; charcoal #1E2523 for text — never pure black; accents
limited to forest green #4C6759 and sage #A9B59D; subtle film grain over the
whole piece; generous whitespace; raw premium apothecary-outdoor mood; flat
editorial design, no drop shadows, no gradients, no glossy effects.

NEGATIVE: pure white background, pure black text, neon or saturated colors,
postcard-turquoise water, colorful filled icons, emoji, HDR, plastic 3D look,
decorative tropical leaves, palm tree clichés, extra logos, watermarks,
